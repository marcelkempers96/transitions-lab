# Who We Serve

*Five kinds of organisation come to the Lab, each with a different decision to make. What holds them together is the same underlying ask: an independent read of what a technology or programme actually does to real people.*

Different audiences arrive with different questions. Each usually needs more than one of the Lab's services, so every card below names the mix that fits.

<div class="target-card c-coral" id="companies">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>Companies &amp; innovators</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p><strong>The problem.</strong> You have a product that works, and now you need to know how it lands somewhere new. Another region, another country, another kind of user, before you commit to scaling. General satisfaction scores will not tell you who adopts, who does not, and why. The risk is spending a scaling budget on an assumption.</p>
    <p><strong>What the Lab does.</strong> Field evidence that turns an expansion decision into a reasoned one, then continuous measurement once you enter. We read your strategy through the <a href="/brw">BRW framework</a> to show which barriers you are bypassing, repurposing, or weakening, and where the fit breaks down.</p>
    <p><strong>Which services fit.</strong> <a href="/entering-a-new-context">Entering a new context</a> for the go/no-go, then <a href="/measuring-change">Measuring change</a> once you are live. Read the <a href="/case-roam">Roam case study</a> for a real engagement.</p>
    <a class="target-cta" href="/entering-a-new-context">Start with entry research →</a>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="target-card c-cobalt" id="funders">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>Funders &amp; public bodies</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p><strong>The problem.</strong> You fund a portfolio, and you need to know which investments are actually working, in a form that travels across very different projects and holds up under scrutiny. Self-reported success from the projects themselves is rarely comparable and never independent.</p>
    <p><strong>What the Lab does.</strong> We measure what a portfolio actually changes, for whom, and through what pathway, against internationally standard evaluation criteria, and we do it independently of the grantee. Instruments are standardised, so a one-off assessment becomes a trajectory you can manage over time.</p>
    <p><strong>Which services fit.</strong> <a href="/for-funders">For Funders</a> for the portfolio-verification frame and SROI, <a href="/measuring-change">Measuring change</a> for the underlying method, and <a href="/reporting-to-funders">Reporting to funders</a> for the presentation to a board or upstream funder.</p>
    <a class="target-cta" href="/for-funders">For Funders →</a>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="target-card c-butter" id="consortia">
  <div class="target-head">
    <img class="target-icon" src="/assets/icons/icon-eu.png" alt="" aria-hidden="true">
    <div class="target-head-text">
      <h3>European consortia</h3>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p><strong>The problem.</strong> Your Grant Agreement commits the consortium to monitoring, evaluation, and a proper dissemination and exploitation plan, and the funder expects reach, uptake, and proof that what was promised was delivered. It is easy to set up channels and hard to show they reached anyone. Done late, it becomes a scramble at the final review.</p>
    <p><strong>What the Lab does.</strong> We act as the independent measurement and dissemination partner: a theory of change and indicator framework set up at the start, a baseline captured before the work begins, continuous monitoring and midline reads while it runs, and an endline evaluation with dissemination that genuinely reaches its audiences.</p>
    <p><strong>Which services fit.</strong> <a href="/reporting-to-funders">Reporting to funders</a> is the primary offer for European projects. It is fed by the field method set out in <a href="/measuring-change">Measuring change</a>.</p>
    <a class="target-cta" href="/reporting-to-funders">Reporting to funders →</a>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="target-card c-forest" id="ngos">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>NGOs &amp; programmes</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p><strong>The problem.</strong> You run a programme on the ground and you need to know, truthfully, whether it is working and for whom, not a report written to reassure a board or a donor. You need someone who can reach the people you serve, in their language and their context, and report what they actually find.</p>
    <p><strong>What the Lab does.</strong> Rigorous primary research in the settings where it is hardest to do well: low-connectivity, multilingual, informal. We read impact across reach, depth, and experience, so the finding is not just how many were touched, but how materially things changed and what the change feels like to the people inside it, including who was left out.</p>
    <p><strong>Which services fit.</strong> <a href="/measuring-change">Measuring change</a> is the primary offer, extended into <a href="/reporting-to-funders">Reporting to funders</a> when the evidence has to travel upstream. Field cases: <a href="/case-mimaji">MiMaji</a>, <a href="/case-pyropower">Pyropower</a>.</p>
    <a class="target-cta" href="/measuring-change">Measuring change →</a>

<div class="target-card c-plum" id="researchers">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>Research teams</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p><strong>The problem.</strong> You have a strong research design and need a partner who can carry it into difficult field contexts with real technical understanding of the systems being studied, not a survey firm that treats the technology as a black box.</p>
    <p><strong>What the Lab does.</strong> Social-science fieldcraft and genuine technical literacy in the same study. Trained researchers who know the place and the language, paired with people who understand how the systems actually work. We can run the fieldwork, design the instruments, or co-author the analysis, and we hold the work to a standard that survives peer scrutiny.</p>
    <p><strong>Which services fit.</strong> <a href="/measuring-change">Measuring change</a> for the field method and analysis; <a href="/entering-a-new-context">Entering a new context</a> when the collaboration is about a specific market or setting. See published tools in <a href="/resources">Resources</a>.</p>
