  </div>

We measure what a portfolio changes, for whom, and through what pathway - against standard evaluation criteria, independently of the grantee. Standardised instruments turn one-off assessments into trajectories you can manage. Every figure on the dashboard has been back-checked.</p>

<div class="target-card c-forest" id="ngos">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>NGOs &amp; programmes</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p class="lede lede-serif">Whether the programme is working, and for whom. Not a report written to reassure the board.</p>
    <p>We reach the people you serve, in their language and their context, and report what they actually find. We work where conventional research fails: low-connectivity, multilingual, informal, dispersed. Impact is read across reach, depth and experience - how many were touched, how materially things changed, and who was left out.</p>
    <p>Field cases: <a href="/case-mimaji">MiMaji</a> in Nairobi, <a href="/case-pyropower">Pyropower</a> in Lombok.</p>

  </div>
