# Who We Serve

*Five kinds of organisation come to the Lab. Each with a different decision in front of them, each needing evidence they can act on.*

Different audiences arrive with different questions. Most engagements combine more than one of the Lab's services. Every card below names the mix that tends to fit, and links you straight into it.

<div class="target-card c-coral" id="companies">
  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>Companies &amp; innovators</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p class="lede">A product that works in one market doesn't automatically work in the next. Before you commit a scaling budget to an assumption, we go and see.</p>
    <p>The signal from a new market is usually ambiguous: are those early sales real demand, or the founder's network? Do the segments from home even exist here? Is the payment stack the one your ops team assumed? We reach the people whose behaviour will decide the outcome and report what they actually do, want, and can afford. We read the strategy through the <a href="/brw">BRW framework</a> to name the barriers you are bypassing, repurposing, or weakening, and where the fit breaks.</p>
    <p>Most engagements start with an entry read and continue as measurement once you're live. See the <a href="/case-roam">mobility case study</a> for what that looks like end to end.</p>

  </div>

    <p>Self-reported success from grantees is rarely comparable and never independent. We measure what a portfolio genuinely changes, for whom, and through what pathway, against internationally standard evaluation criteria, and we do it independently of the grantee. Standardised instruments turn a one-off assessment into a trajectory you can manage over time. Every figure on the dashboard is one someone has been sent to check.</p>

  <div class="target-head">
    <h3>NGOs &amp; programmes</h3>
  </div>
  <div class="target-body">
    <p class="lede">Whether the programme is working, and for whom, not a report written to reassure a board.</p>
    <p>You need someone who can reach the people you serve, in their language and their context, and report what they actually find. We work in the settings that defeat conventional research: low-connectivity, multilingual, informal, dispersed. Impact is read across reach, depth, and experience, so the finding is not just how many were touched, but how materially things changed, what the change feels like to the people inside it, and who was left out.</p>
    <p>Field cases: <a href="/case-mimaji">MiMaji</a> in Nairobi, <a href="/case-pyropower">Pyropower</a> in Lombok.</p>
