# Expertise

*The transitions the Lab knows deeply. Each is a place where a technology is meeting people at scale, in context, and where the human questions are as hard as the technical ones.*

<!-- IMAGE expertise-hero: hero photo | Composite or single strong field image spanning the Lab's domains: a rider, a solar panel, a water point, a farmer, a screen. Documentary, real, on location. | Full-width hero under the intro. -->

The Lab does not claim to know every sector. It knows a specific set of transitions in depth, the ones where its method (social science first, technical literacy alongside, independence throughout) has the most to offer. These are the areas where our own research, our fieldwork, and the wider evidence base we draw on come together.

Each page below sets out what the transition is, why it is hard, the topics we are actively interested in, and the institutional and scientific knowledge our understanding is built on. We do not work *for* the organisations we reference. We draw *on* the public evidence they produce, alongside our own.

A note that runs through all of them: every transition is also a question of who is served and who is left out. Equity is not a separate expertise. It is the lens we bring to each of these.

**§ 1 / The eight areas**

## Where the Lab works

[**E-Mobility & Transport Infrastructure →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-e-mobility.html)
How electric and shared transport crosses from novelty to default in markets built around petrol and the informal ride.

[**Energy Access & Off-Grid Systems →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-energy.html)
How decentralised energy reaches the hundreds of millions still beyond the grid, and whether it reaches them fairly.

[**Water & Sanitation →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-water.html)
How safe water and sanitation are delivered, sustained, and trusted, in the one region where the number of people without access is still rising.

[**Regenerative Agriculture & Agri-Tech →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-agriculture.html)
How smallholders adopt, adapt, and benefit from technologies that turn waste into value and degraded land into productive soil.

[**Local Manufacturing & Supply Chains →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-manufacturing.html)
How productive capacity is built where things are used, and whether local assembly and value addition actually reach the people they promise to.

[**AI & Digital Systems →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-ai-digital.html)
How artificial intelligence and digital tools land in contexts they were not designed for, and whether they close divides or widen them.

[**Financial Inclusion & Payment Systems →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-finance.html)
How payment, credit, and financing systems carry, or block, every other transition that depends on people being able to pay over time.

[**Climate Resilience & Ecosystems →**](https://www.transitionslab.org/expertise-climate.html)
How communities and ecosystems adapt to a changing climate, across reefs, forests, wetlands, and biodiversity, and how monitoring becomes trusted enough to drive action.
