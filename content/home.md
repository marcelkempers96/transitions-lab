# Aligning technology with the people it is meant to serve.

*Transitions Lab is an independent research team working on the ground, building deep understanding of socio-technical transitions, so that human values and lived experience shape how technologies arrive in the world.*

We go to where a transition is actually happening, the workshop, the field, the household, and we study what a technology does once real people live with it. Then we feed that evidence back to the innovators, institutions, and communities who can act on it. **People first; the technology second; the place always.**
