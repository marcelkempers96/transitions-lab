# Field Research

*Primary research in the settings where it is hardest to do well.*

---

## Where we work

Low-connectivity, multilingual, informal, dispersed. Through researchers already part of the context. Recent ground: [electric transport in Nairobi](/case-roam), [decentralised clean energy in Lombok](/case-pyropower), [water transparency in Nairobi](/case-mimaji).

## Ethics and rigour

Informed, recorded, revocable consent. Anonymisation by default. Sampling, translation and quality control managed end to end.

Held to the Belmont Report, the WMA Declaration of Helsinki, IPA and J-PAL, EU GDPR, and the ESRC Framework for Research Ethics. See [Research Ethics](/ethics).

Commissioned work stays private unless the client chooses to publish; the [case studies](/case-studies) are the exceptions.
