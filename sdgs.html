Research by Sustainable Development Goal

The Lab's programmes claim eleven of the seventeen Sustainable Development Goals. Only where a programme sits behind them, and only with the modest phrase the framework allows: contributes to.

The Sustainable Development Goals are a common language for orienting research to public purpose. They are also easy to abuse: a page claiming all seventeen goals claims none of them. Where the Lab does not have work behind a claim, it says so plainly.

For the six programmes themselves, see Programmes.

The eleven goals we contribute to

SDG 1 · No Poverty

  • No Poverty

Contributed to by Financial Inclusion. Asset finance, default risk and the design of instruments that reach the households infrastructure otherwise stops at.

SDG 2 · Zero Hunger

  • Zero Hunger

Contributed to by Agriculture & Ecosystems. Waste-to-value, on-farm technology adoption, and what the monitoring behind a payment actually costs a smallholder farm.

SDG 6 · Clean Water and Sanitation

  • Clean Water and Sanitation

Contributed to by Energy & Water Systems. Water sources, treatment and rural access alongside delivery, vendors and payment - from Delft-adjacent transboundary and drought-stressed catchments through to the region where the number of people without safe water is still rising.

SDG 7 · Affordable and Clean Energy

  • Affordable and Clean Energy

Contributed to by Energy & Water Systems. Mini-grids and off-grid supply, storage and grid integration on a grid that already exists, and who bears the cost of firm capacity.

SDG 8 · Decent Work and Economic Growth

  • Decent Work and Economic Growth

Contributed to by Green Industrialisation & Local Manufacturing, E-Mobility & Transport, and Financial Inclusion. Decent work is the thread through workforce capability, rider economics and asset finance, and it is the substance of pieces such as The Factory Cannot Transition Without the Town and Local Content Is Not the Same as Local Benefit.

SDG 9 · Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

  • Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Contributed to by Green Industrialisation & Local Manufacturing and AI & Digital Systems. Critical raw materials and processing, circular economy, data centre infrastructure and automation.

SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

  • Reduced Inequalities

Contributed to by AI & Digital Systems and Financial Inclusion. Who a benchmarked model fails for, and who is left outside a payment or credit system by design.

SDG 11 · Sustainable Cities and Communities

  • Sustainable Cities and Communities

Contributed to by E-Mobility & Transport and Energy & Water Systems. Urban transport, load, grid integration and the water systems a city depends on.

SDG 12 · Responsible Consumption and Production

  • Responsible Consumption and Production

Contributed to by Green Industrialisation & Local Manufacturing. Circular economy and e-waste, secondary materials, and end-of-life claims that hold up.

SDG 13 · Climate Action

  • Climate Action

Contributed to by E-Mobility & Transport. Where the Lab's climate work is measured through livelihoods rather than through emissions.

SDG 14 · Life Below Water

  • Life Below Water

Contributed to by Agriculture & Ecosystems. Coastal and marine monitoring, community rangers and the Reef Support case study.

SDG 15 · Life on Land

  • Life on Land

Contributed to by Agriculture & Ecosystems. Soil restoration, forest and wetland monitoring, and carbon and biodiversity verification. See the Pyropower case study on smallholder biochar in Lombok - crop waste turned into soil amendment and carbon-market income.

The six we do not claim

Named plainly, so the eleven above hold up

The Lab has no published field research behind these goals, and does not claim them: SDG 3 Good Health and Well-being, SDG 4 Quality Education, SDG 5 Gender Equality, SDG 16 Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions, SDG 17 Partnerships for the Goals.

Gender considerations run through all six programmes as an equity lens, but there is no gender-specific fieldwork behind a headline claim; naming that here is more useful than a bar chart that implies otherwise.
