European Project Impact-Tracking Template

A working framework for measuring the before-and-after impact of a European project, demonstration, or socially innovative action, set up at the start, run while the work is live, closed out with proof.

This template is built to be filled in. It is deliberately light: every field earns its place by feeding a decision or a final claim. Adapt the indicators to your project; keep the structure, because the structure is what makes one project's evidence comparable to another's.

How to use it: complete Part A at kick-off, before any activity begins. Run Part B at the agreed midpoints while the project is live. Complete Part C at close. Parts D and E are designed once and reused throughout.

▢ Did the change happen because of the project, or alongside it? (state the basis) ▢ What else was going on that could explain the result? ▢ Who was left out, and does that change the headline? ▢ What would we measure differently next time?

The willingness to write down these answers is the difference between an evaluation and a press release.

Part D, Reusable design assets (set once)

A one-line, fixed definition for every indicator, so "participant," "user," and "reached" mean the same thing across every site and partner. Incomparable definitions are the most common reason multi-site evidence fragments.

Target sample per site; how respondents are selected; how informed consent is obtained and recorded; how the data is stored. Contact only consented participants.

One row per site, same columns, so results can be read side by side rather than as a pile of separate reports.
