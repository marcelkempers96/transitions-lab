Case studies

Where we've worked on the ground.

How Nairobi's boda-bodas go electric

Nairobi, Kenya · E-Mobility & Transport

Twenty-three interviews with riders, mechanics, a lender and a manufacturer on what actually decides whether the switch to electric holds after the first month.

Read the case →

Turning crop waste into fuel in Lombok

Lombok, Indonesia · Agriculture & Ecosystems · with Pyropower

An open-source kiln that turns rice husks and coconut shells into cooking fuel and soil, tested household by household with the farmers who would run it.

Read the case →

Who watches Nairobi's water?

Nairobi, Kenya · Energy & Water Systems · with MiMaji Foundation

An open data layer for informal-settlement water kiosks, co-designed with the residents who had never been on any water utility's map.

Read the case →

Guarding Lombok's reef with rangers and satellites

Lombok, Indonesia · Marine conservation · with Reef Support

Village rangers, in-water sensors and satellite imagery braided into one trustworthy picture of reef health, and a governance model to defend it.
