Our network

A global team of researchers, analysts, and field partners.

The Lab is a small core in Delft and a wider network of trained field researchers, local analysts, and long-standing partners in the places we work. We do not parachute in, and we do not run studies at arm's length.

01

A Delft core, and a global bench

Our researchers speak the language, know the market, and have context that a fly-in team never accumulates. New geographies are staffed from existing relationships, not job boards.

02

Anchored in the research community

Based alongside TU Delft, the Lab is close to the academic communities that produce the frameworks we use, and open enough to publish its own work back.

03

Partners on the ground

Long-standing engagements with named partners like Roam, MiMaji, Pyropower, and Reef Support, plus a wider bench of local research organisations in East Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Dutch Caribbean.
