What we do

Independent evidence, three ways in.

We reach the people a technology or programme actually meets, measure what changes, and report it honestly.

Entering a new context

Independent field evidence for the decision to enter a new market or launch a new product line. Who adopts, at what price, and which failure modes to name early.
