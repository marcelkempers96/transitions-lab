Expertise

Expertise

Eight transitions the Lab knows in depth. Each is a place where a technology is meeting people at scale, and the human questions are as hard as the technical ones.

We don't claim every sector. We know the ones where our method (social science first, technical literacy alongside, independence throughout) has the most to offer.

A note that runs through all of them

Every transition is also a question of who is served and who is left out. Equity is not a separate expertise. It is the lens we bring to each of these.

Where the Lab works

  • E-Mobility & Transport Infrastructure

    How electric and shared transport crosses from novelty to default in markets built around petrol.

    →

  • Energy Access & Off-Grid Systems

    How decentralised energy reaches the hundreds of millions still beyond the grid, and whether it reaches them fairly.

    →

  • Water & Sanitation

    How safe water and sanitation are delivered, sustained, and trusted, in the region where the number of people without access is still rising.

    →

  • Regenerative Agriculture & Agri-Tech

    How smallholders adopt, adapt, and benefit from technologies that turn waste into value and degraded land into productive soil.

    →

  • Local Manufacturing & Supply Chains

    How productive capacity is built where things are used, and whether local assembly reaches the people it promises to.

    →

  • AI & Digital Systems

    How artificial intelligence and digital tools land in contexts they were not designed for.

    →

  • Financial Inclusion & Payment Systems

    How payment, credit, and financing systems carry, or block, every other transition that depends on people being able to pay over time.

    →

  • Climate Resilience & Ecosystems

    How communities and ecosystems adapt to a changing climate, and how monitoring becomes trusted enough to drive action.

    →
