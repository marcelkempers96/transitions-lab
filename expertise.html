Programmes

Six transitions the Lab knows in depth. Each is a place where a technology is meeting people at scale, and the human questions are as hard as the technical ones.

Every transition is also a question of who is served and who is left out.

Research can also be browsed by Sustainable Development Goal.

Where the Lab works

  • Green Industrialisation & Local Manufacturing

    How productive capacity is built where things are used, who captures the value, and whether the workforce capability a plant assumes actually arrives with it.

    Research areas: Critical raw materials and processing · Circular economy and e-waste · Assembly, suppliers and workforce capability

    →

  • E-Mobility & Transport Infrastructure

    How electric and shared transport crosses from novelty to default, and what it does to the livelihoods built on the vehicles it replaces.

    Research areas: Rider and operator economics · Batteries, charging and swap infrastructure

    →

  • Energy & Water Systems

    How energy and water systems are built, paid for and relied on, where the network reaches and where it never did.

    Research areas: Mini-grids and off-grid supply · Renewables, storage and grid integration · Water sources, treatment and rural access · Water delivery, vendors and payment

    →

  • Agriculture & Ecosystems

    How farmers and fishers adopt and benefit from new technology, and what the monitoring behind a payment actually costs them.

    Research areas: Waste-to-value and soil restoration · On-farm technology adoption · Coastal and marine monitoring · Carbon and biodiversity verification

    →

  • AI & Digital Systems

    How artificial intelligence and digital tools land in contexts they were not designed for.

    Research areas: Data centres and digital infrastructure · Automation and skills displacement

    →

  • Financial Inclusion

    How payment, credit and financing systems carry, or block, every other transition that depends on someone being able to pay over time, and who is left outside them.

    Research areas: Asset finance and default risk · Who gets included and who does not

    →

An independent research team that studies how technologies meet real people, and turns what it finds into evidence institutions and innovators can act on.
